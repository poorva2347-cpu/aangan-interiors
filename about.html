About the studio

A more thoughtful way to live.

AANGAN Interiors is a residential design studio that believes a home should be shaped by the people who live in it — their routines, their rituals and their memories.

The name

आँगन

Aangan — the courtyard at the heart of an Indian home.

For generations the aangan was where the day began and ended: tea in the morning sun, children playing, festivals gathering the whole family. It was open to the sky yet completely private — the place that made a house feel like home.

We named the studio after it because that is what we try to design in every room: a centre of everyday life.

What we believe

Five values guide every project.

  • People before trends

    We design for how you live, not for what is fashionable this year.

  • Beauty with everyday function

    Storage, light and movement are solved before decoration.

  • Thoughtful material choices

    Natural, honest materials that age gracefully.

  • Attention to detail

    Joinery, edges and junctions considered at every scale.

  • Spaces that feel personal

    Your objects, your stories, your home.

Our approach

Listen first. Design second.

We begin every project by understanding the household — who wakes first, where shoes are kept, how guests are welcomed. Only then do we plan layouts, choose materials and draw details. The result is a home that looks considered and simply works.
